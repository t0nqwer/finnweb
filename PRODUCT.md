# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Thai SME owners and small teams who need to launch a credible web presence quickly without technical complexity. They are often time-constrained, mobile-first in daily workflow, and need clear tools that help them publish and convert leads fast.

## Product Purpose
FinnWeb exists to help Thai small businesses build and launch high-performing websites and landing pages quickly, then convert visitors into leads through practical conversion tools. Success means users can go from idea to published site with confidence, speed, and clear business value.

## Brand Personality
เร็ว, มืออาชีพ, สวย. The experience should feel fast and decisive, polished and trustworthy, and visually memorable without becoming noisy or hard to use.

## Anti-references
- Generic template-marketplace aesthetics that feel interchangeable and low-trust
- Overloaded SaaS dashboards where visual effects distract from primary conversion goals
- Slow, heavy animation patterns that reduce responsiveness or block content comprehension
- Bright-on-bright low-contrast marketing pages that hurt readability, especially on mobile

## Design Principles
- Lead with outcomes: show users how quickly they can launch and collect leads, not just feature lists.
- Motion with intent: animation should reinforce hierarchy, feedback, and flow progression, never decoration for its own sake.
- Thai-first clarity: prioritize Thai copy readability, scanning, and confidence at every screen density.
- Premium practicality: visual quality should feel modern and polished while preserving direct, low-friction decision paths.
- Consistent trust cues: pricing, plan messaging, and conversion actions should remain stable and unambiguous across surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the default baseline for customer-facing experiences. Maintain strong color contrast and legible Thai typography, including line-height suitable for Thai-heavy text blocks. Support reduced-motion preferences by providing non-animated or toned-down alternatives for all meaningful motion effects.
